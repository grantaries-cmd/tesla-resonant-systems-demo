Energy accounting convention

  • Input — work delivered by the declared source (drive voltage, applied force, or fluid torque on the rotor).
  • Stored — field, elastic/kinetic, or rotor kinetic energy currently in the model.
  • Loss — copper heat, viscous damping, or bearing loss.
  • Load — energy delivered to the declared load.
  • Residual — ΔU − Win + Wloss + Wload: numerical integration error only.

Evidence labels used in the dossier: [D] executed synthetic computation · [M] mathematical interpretation · [E] external measurement · [P] proposal. Everything in this app is [D] or [M]; no [E] device or efficiency result exists.

Reading this model

dossier v2.0
  • Strong coupling splits the response into ω₋ and ω₊. Driving at the isolated frequency ω₀ is not automatically optimal for load power.
  • Resonance cancels a reactance at one frequency; it does not remove resistance. Copper loss I²R stays in the ledger.
  • A finite-window output/input ratio includes the change in stored energy. Compare period-averaged powers near steady state.
  • κ → 1 makes the ideal inductance matrix singular, not a route to unlimited gain; the slider stops at 0.90.
  • Lumped comparator only: no distributed winding, corona, streamers, spark gap, or terminal-voltage prediction.

Reading this model

dossier v2.0
  • At ω = ω₀ displacement lags force by π/2 while force and velocity are aligned, so a non-zero displacement phase is not a failure.
  • The displacement peak sits at ω₀√(1−2ζ²) ≈ 0.9996 ω₀; velocity and power responses have their own criteria.
  • In steady state the average input equals damping dissipation. Energy does not accumulate indefinitely.
  • An undamped oscillator driven at its mode grows without bound in the linear model; that is not stable, usable operation.

Controls

m = 1 kg · ks = 100 N/m · cm = 0.4 N·s/m · F₀ = 0.1 N. Transients decay with τ = 2m/cm = 5 s, so the live amplitude approaches the analytic value after several τ.

Reading this model

dossier v2.0
  • The torque curve is imposed. Transferred power τ·Ω peaks at η_cap·P_flow at Ω = Ω_free/2 by construction.
  • Viscous shear transfers angular momentum and also dissipates; no-slip coupling is not loss-free adhesion.
  • Shaft speed is a first-order spin-up to Ω_ss. No oscillatory mode is modelled, so no resonance is claimed for the rotor.
  • Real performance needs fluid equations, gaps, roughness, leakage, bearings and calibrated measurement.

Controls

Jr = 0.1 kg·m² · bearing 0.01 N·m·s · load 0.04 N·m·s · ηcap = 0.65 · Ωfree = 100 rad/s. Illustrative lumped control/energy test; must not be used for device sizing.
